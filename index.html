3 rd week

September 7th – Grammar Correction & Article Rules Miss arrived a little late today. I brought my printed family photo as instructed. We started with a quick activity: writing down 10 words that describe or remind us of 'Family' (like harmonious, warm, and love). Then, we swapped notebooks with our seatmates and wrote 6 sentences using their words. Miss walked around to check our work, correct grammar mistakes, and help us improve. After that, Miss explained the rules for using Articles (A, An, The): 'A' and 'An' (Indefinite): Used for general things. We use 'A' before consonant sounds (a family, a photo) and 'An' before vowel sounds (an uncle, an idea). 'The' (Definite): Used for specific things (the photo on the desk) or unique things (the environment). Pronunciation tip: We pronounce 'the' as 'De' before consonant sounds (the family) and as 'Di' before vowel sounds (the environment)."

4 th week —

September 14th – Daily Activities & Adverb of Frequency. We practiced making simple sentences about daily activities using the Simple Present Tense. Miss explained Adverb of Frequency—words like always, usually, often, sometimes, occasionally, rarely, and never, including their levels and positions. She taught us that we place them before the main verb (like 'I always study') and after verb to be (like 'He is usually late'). During class, we created our own sentences and Miss corrected our grammar one by one. For our assignment, we were given 18 simple sentences, and we had to choose at least 10 to change into questions, ask our friends, and record their answers.

5 th week — 

September 21st – Past Habits & Interactive Quiz We learned the Simple Past Tense to talk about past activities, focusing on positive patterns, negative patterns (did not + Verb 1), and questions (Did + Subject + Verb 1), as well as regular vs irregular verbs. We practiced writing about what we did the day before (Sunday) with direct corrections from Miss. We also learned two additional expressions: 'Used to + Verb 1' for past habits that we don't do anymore, and 'Wanted to be' for childhood dreams! At the end, we played an interactive quiz on the screen filled with blank dots where each vertical row had to raise hands to fill in the blanks. I volunteered to answer, and I got it right on the first try without any corrections
